# Private commercial review list

**Internal — not for publication.** The same checklist is in the admin under
*Packages & prices → Private review*, where it can be ticked off.

## Status at handoff

- **Published on the website:** the original monthly prices (unchanged), package names,
  short descriptions based on the original price list, add-on names and prices,
  “Recommended” badges on Dominance and Vision.
- **Saved as an unpublished draft (approval state: Proposed):** every revised delivery
  scope from the service proposal — quantities, limits, response windows, website terms,
  exclusions, general package terms, add-on scopes — in Armenian, English and Russian.
- The draft can be reviewed with **Preview** (signed in) on any pricing page. Nothing in it
  is visible to visitors until it is approved and published.

## Decisions to confirm before publishing the draft

1. **Capacity and margin** for shoots (2 h Dominance, 3 h Gold), the 4 edited Gold videos,
   community management (up to 150 interactions/month), Meta campaign management
   (1 / 2 / 3 active campaigns), SEO articles (2 Vision, 6 Authority), technical hours
   (3 / 5 / 8 per month) and outreach (10 pitches/month).
2. **Counts:** feed pieces 8 / 10 / 16; story frames 8 / 10 / 16; edited photos 8 / 15;
   indexable URLs 25 / 50 / 100; keywords 50 / 100 / 200; optimised pages 5 / 10 / 20;
   competitors 3 / 5; AI-search query sample 10; Highlights covers 5.
3. **Working hours and response targets:** Mon–Fri 10:00–18:00 Armenia time;
   acknowledgment within 2 working days (standard) / 1 working day (priority);
   Gold community replies aimed within 1 working day.
4. **Language limit:** client campaign content in one agreed language.
5. **Ad-spend separation** and the list of separately charged external costs.
6. **Website Creation:** 5 pages, 15–20 working-day timeline, 50% / 50% payment schedule,
   30-day defect support, exclusions list.
7. **Add-ons:** reel shoot durations (1 h single / 2 h for the 4-pack), 45-second limit,
   article length 800–1,200 words, landing page up to 6 sections.
8. **Prices were not revalidated** against the new workload. The original prices are
   unchanged; check delivery cost per package before approving.
9. SEO Fundamental stays **unavailable for 9 and 12 months**, as in the original price list.

## Notes from implementation

- Savings are calculated from prices ((1-month fee − selected fee) × months). All savings
  printed in the original PDF match this calculation.
- “Save up to” on cards uses the largest real saving for that package.
- Combined plans keep their own prices (Business Boost ≠ Dominance + Vision fees).
- Paid SEO-article add-ons are offered on top of plan articles and the estimate says so;
  plan articles are never charged again.
- Published website copy avoids the proposed terms (e.g. it says “regular reporting”
  rather than “monthly reports”). After approval you may want to strengthen page copy.
- No client work, testimonials, results, credentials, biographies, awards, address or
  social profiles were invented. Instagram is hidden until a link is entered.
